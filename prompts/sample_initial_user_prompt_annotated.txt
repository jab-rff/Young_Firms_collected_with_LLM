Appendix-style sample initial discovery user prompt
Source sample: data/discovery/snowball_round_005_bucket_runs.jsonl

Notes for appendix use:
- This is a sample of the user prompt used in the first web-search stage.
- The purpose of this stage is broad discovery, not final validation.
- Values prefixed with `*` depend on the selected search bucket.
- The current version of the script defines 49 buckets in total:
- `sector` buckets: 10
- `destination` buckets: 15
- `city_pair` buckets: 9
- `cross_bucket` buckets: 15
- `mechanism` buckets: 0 in the current version
- `source_style` buckets: 0 in the current version
- Sector buckets cover: `biotech`, `SaaS/software`, `fintech`, `gaming`, `medtech`, `logistics`, `cleantech`, `retail/consumer`, `hospitality`, and `design`.
- Destination buckets cover: `US`, `UK`, `Germany`, `Sweden`, `Switzerland`, `Netherlands`, `Ireland`, `Portugal`, `Italy`, `Spain`, `Singapore`, `Canada`, `China`, `UAE`, and `South Africa`.
- City-pair buckets cover: `Copenhagen to abroad`, `Aarhus to abroad`, `Denmark to London`, `Denmark to New York City`, `Denmark to Boston`, `Denmark to San Francisco`, `Denmark to Los Angeles`, `Denmark to Paris`, and `Denmark to Berlin`.
- Cross buckets combine a sector with a destination. In the current version there are 15 such combinations.
- In this sample, the chosen bucket is `*sector:biotech`.
